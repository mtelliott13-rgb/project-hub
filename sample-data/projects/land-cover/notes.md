# Land Cover Change Detection

Remote sensing class project: classify land cover from two satellite scenes five years apart and map what changed. (Sample notes, made up.)

- Classes: water, forest, grassland, cropland, developed.
- Supervised random forest on hand-drawn training polygons; hold out 30% for the accuracy check.
- Current overall accuracy 86%. Cropland and grassland get confused in early spring scenes.
- Report due Dec 10: methods, confusion matrix, change map, area-change table.
